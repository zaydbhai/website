Highway Bridge

This reliable bridge represents the pinnacle of modern civil design, combining aesthetic appeal with functional commuting spaces. The project was completed in 2013 after 18 months of careful planning and execution.

Project Overview

The Modern Residence project was designed for a city looking for a sustainable, capital-efficient bridge that didn't compromise on style. The 2 kilometer long bridge features a cable held design, central support tower, and seamless walkway support.

We utilized advanced construction techniques and materials to ensure the bridge achieved a 5-star sustainability rating.

Challenges & Solutions

The sloping site presented challenges for foundation work. Our engineering team developed an innovative cantilevered design that not only solved the structural issues but also created dramatic viewing platforms that take advantage of the property's elevated position.

We also faced supply chain delays for certain imported materials due to global circumstances. Our project managers sourced alternative local materials that met our quality standards while maintaining the design integrity, actually reducing the carbon footprint of the project.
