Our 30-Year Journey

1994

Foundation

Al-Mehar Construction was established with a vision to transform Pakistan's construction industry through quality workmanship and ethical practices.

2002

First Major Project

Successfully completed the Doctor Essa Labotory, establishing our reputation for delivering large-scale projects on time and within budget.

2010

Expansion

Expanded operations to include industrial construction and infrastructure projects, growing our team to over 200 skilled professionals.

2024

Present Day

Now with over 500 completed projects, we continue to build Pakistan's future while maintaining our commitment to quality and integrity.

Message From Our CEO

For three decades, Al-Mehar Construction has stood as a testament to the power of strong foundations—both in our structures and in our values. As a civil engineer with over 30 years of experience, I've always believed that buildings are more than just concrete and steel; they are the spaces where lives unfold, businesses thrive, and communities grow.

Our philosophy, "Strong Foundations, Lasting Structures," guides every project we undertake. It represents our commitment to not only constructing physically sound buildings but also building lasting relationships with our clients, partners, and the communities we serve.

I'm proud of our diverse, cooperative team whose expertise and dedication have made us one of Pakistan's most trusted construction firms. From commercial high-rises to residential communities, each project carries our signature quality and attention to detail.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to innovation, sustainability, and excellence in everything we build.

Engr. Muhammad Aqeel Sarwar
