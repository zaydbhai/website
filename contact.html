Frequently Asked Questions

What types of construction projects do you handle?
We handle a wide range of construction projects including residential buildings, commercial complexes, industrial facilities, and renovation projects. Our expertise covers everything from small home renovations to large-scale commercial developments.
How long does a typical construction project take?
Project timelines vary based on scope, size, and complexity. A small renovation might take 4-8 weeks, while a custom home could take 6-12 months. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation and maintain regular communication throughout the project.
Do you provide free estimates?
Yes, we offer free, no-obligation estimates for all potential projects. After understanding your requirements, we'll provide a detailed breakdown of costs and timelines so you can make an informed decision.
Are you licensed and insured?
Absolutely. Al-Mehar Construction is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We prioritize safety and compliance in all our projects, ensuring peace of mind for our clients.
