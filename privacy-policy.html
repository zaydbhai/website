Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

Al-Mehar Construction ("we," "our," or "the Company") respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting the confidentiality of our website visitors and clients looking to book a consultation. This Privacy Policy documents our data management practices in strict alignment with the Pakistan Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and international standards of data processing transparently.

2. Information Collected Through the Site

We do not operate backend user databases, nor do we retain your information on automated client relationship management tools via this web server. The data handled is completely confined to details explicitly provided by you inside our "Book a Consultation" module, which includes:

  • Your Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Subject Line
  • The descriptive contents of your consultation inquiry

3. The Forwarding Operational Model

To eliminate data tracking vulnerabilities, our portal operates a strict direct-forwarding processing configuration:

  • No Server Logs: Your form communications are never written to, logged by, or saved on cloud web databases.
  • Executive Level Direct Flow: Content entries are systematically converted to encrypted email formats and routed directly onto the communication terminal of our Chief Executive Officer (CEO).
  • Internal Review: The CEO processes individual consultation entries to delegate handling down to specified internal staff operatives to carry out the response process.

4. Processing Intent

We restrict parsing operations exclusively toward answering structural design requests or answering generic site development queries. We execute no marketing tracking newsletters, nor do we distribute information footprints into external broker marketplaces.

5. Security Controls and Retention Profiles

By routing data into isolated secure corporate email infrastructure rather than keeping data on open network interfaces, risk structures are heavily minimized. Correspondence trails are handled matching standard internal compliance profiles required under Pakistani construction corporate bylaws.

6. Your Control Privileges

Under local code metrics, users keep structural verification powers over past communications. You maintain full permission configurations to update details or file a petition requiring deletion of historic messaging records by reaching us via our listed communications interface.
